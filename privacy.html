Privacy Policy

Release Radar Sync is a personal tool used by a single person to manage their own YouTube playlist.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it creates and edits one playlist on the owner's own YouTube account (adding and removing songs).

What it collects

Nothing. It does not collect, store, share or sell data about any other person. Sign-in details stay on the owner's own computer.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
